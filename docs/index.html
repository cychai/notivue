01

为什么是 Notivue Why Notivue

视频适合观看，
却不总是适合学习。 Video is made to watch,
not always to study.

Notivue 在视频旁边建立一个安静的学习工作区。字幕成为可搜索的文本，播放进度变成可返回的时间坐标，AI 帮助你理解结构，而你的笔记最终成为自己的资料。 Notivue adds a quiet learning workspace beside the video. Transcripts become searchable text, playback becomes a navigable timeline, AI reveals the structure, and your notes become material you own.

03

本地优先，不是附加选项 Local-first is not an add-on

你的学习资料，
留在你的浏览器里。 Your learning material
stays in your browser.

Notivue 没有账号系统、服务端、分析埋点或遥测。设置、API Key、字幕缓存、AI 结果和笔记均保存在 Chrome 本地存储。 Notivue has no accounts, backend, analytics, or telemetry. Settings, API keys, transcript cache, AI results, and notes live in Chrome local storage.

本地存储Local storage

主动调用On-demand AI

只有当你主动使用 AI 功能时，当前任务所需文本才会直接发送到你选择的 OpenAI-compatible 模型服务。 Only when you invoke an AI feature is the text required for that task sent directly to your chosen OpenAI-compatible provider.

04

从播放到沉淀 From playback to lasting notes

三个动作，
建立自己的理解。 Three moves to build
your own understanding.

Notivue 不替你学习。它减少在播放器、字幕和笔记工具之间切换的阻力，让注意力留在内容本身。 Notivue does not learn for you. It removes the friction between player, transcript, and notes so your attention stays with the ideas.

  1. 1

    打开视频Open a video

    在带官方字幕的 YouTube 视频页打开 Notivue。Open Notivue on a YouTube video with official captions.

  2. 2

    阅读与追问Read and inquire

    搜索字幕、切换双语、生成章节，或解释选中的内容。Search, switch languages, generate chapters, or explain a selection.

  3. 3

    记录与导出Note and export

    写下带时间戳的笔记，导出完整 Markdown 学习资料。Write timestamped notes and export a complete Markdown learning document.
